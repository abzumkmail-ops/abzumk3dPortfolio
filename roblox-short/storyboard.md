# Storyboard v2 + Production Plan

Status: DRAFT for approval. Timestamps are estimates at ~209 wpm; final timings will be snapped to the real voiceover's word timestamps.
Output: 1080×1920, 60 fps, H.264 CRF 17, AAC 192 kbps.

**Visual rule for avatars:** everyone is a blocky cartoon person; their *avatars* are 60% height, hover 10 px off the ground, have a soft cyan hologram outline glow and a floating name tag. That way avatars are always readable as "the game following you".

## B. Timer behavior (story device)
- Hidden until shot 8. Pops in at 0:10 (dark pill, white text, stopwatch icon, top center).
- Steps on story beats: 0:09 (9), 0:08 (10), 0:07 (11), 0:05 RED (12), 0:04 (13), 0:03/0:02 (14), freezes during the slow-mo (15–16), 0:00 + flash (17), then flies off.

## D. Asset list
**Engine (JS/Canvas, deterministic `seek(t)`, `READY`, `END`, `EV`)**
- rig.js: skeleton, pivots, pose blending, spring/overshoot easing
- face.js: 12 expressions, blink scheduler (2.5–4 s jittered), brows, 7 mouth shapes + lip-sync from word timestamps
- actions.js: idle, walk, run, jump, trip, tackle/dive, breakdance, wing-flap, draw-heart, sit, head-turn, hold-sign, fist-bump
- camera.js: push/pull, punch-in, whip pan (with motion blur), tracking, shake, parallax
- fx.js: sparkles, dust puffs, confetti, hearts, glow trails, speed lines, tears, flash frame, vignette
- ui.js: timer pill (normal/red), world-rule card, wallet counter, reward pop ("MATCH FOUND!", "PHOTO TAKEN"), name tags
- captions.js: word-synced 1–4 word chunks, Lilita One, white + black stroke, color emphasis, pop/overshoot/tilt
- timeline.js: the 22 shots above as data

**Backgrounds (layered vector, cached):** school hallway (lockers, posters, windows, floor), classroom (board, desks, windows, photographer tripod), kitchen, bedroom (night).

**Audio:** ElevenLabs voiceover (4 voices) → faster-whisper word alignment. Music: original procedurally composed upbeat chiptune-pop loop (+ soft pad version for shots 15–16), made in Python — no licensing risk. SFX: synthesized in Python (ticks, whooshes, pops, chimes, shutter, bonk, alarm, sparkle, scratch).
