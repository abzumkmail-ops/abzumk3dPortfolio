## Logline
Every kid's Roblox avatar walks beside them and copies what they *really* feel. Nubi's is a default noob, class photo is in 10 seconds — and his avatar can't stop drawing hearts at Lily.

## Beat map
- 0–3 s HOOK: Nubi walks into class, noob avatar waddles behind him.
- 3–8 s RULES: rule card, teacher announces photo, timer pill appears at 0:10; Brayden's dragon-winged avatar mocks.
- 8–15 s FIRST WIN: shop UI, buy sunglasses, "+1% COOL" pop.
- 15–24 s ESCALATION: backflip, arm-wrestle vs dragon, "LEGENDARY NOOB" badge, class cheers.
- 24–33 s TWIST: Lily enters (soft music drop), avatar draws giant hearts; Tobi's line.
- 33–43 s PANIC: timer turns red, tackle, slow-mo chase to Lily's desk, 3…2…
- 43–50 s PAYOFF: flash, freeze-frame photo; Lily's avatar is a noob with a "NUBI" sign.
- 50–55 s LOVE + LOOP: two noob avatars hold hands; camera pulls back to the photo pinned on the classroom wall, which matches the first shot's background.

## Claim review
No real Roblox mechanics are asserted. "Robux" and "default noob" are used as everyday player vocabulary; avatars following you, feelings-copying and the shop are fictional story devices.
